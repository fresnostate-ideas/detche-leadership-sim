The opportunity is real.

Your university has XR equipment, interested faculty, an underused lab, and no shared plan for what comes next. The provost wants leadership. What will you do?

YOUR ROLEDirector of Academic Technology

You report directly to the provost, lead instructional design and emerging learning technology, and manage a capable—but stretched—team. Dr. Park coordinates requests for additional Academic Affairs funding.

THE RULESFour decisions. No do-overs.

Some consequences arrive later. Not all events are under your control. There is no leadership score.
