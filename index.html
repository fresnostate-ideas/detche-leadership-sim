LEADERSHIP PRACTICE

What would you choose again?

Which consequence surprised you? Where did you exercise judgment, and where did circumstance make the decision for you? What would you change—knowing you can't know everything in advance?

Leadership isn’t about always knowing the right thing to do. It’s about knowing what matters to you, understanding what matters to others, and exercising judgment when those commitments come into conflict.

Even then, you may not get the outcome you hoped for, the recognition you expected, or the support you believed you had earned. You may not always be your best self, either.

The work of leadership is navigating those tensions—and learning from the consequences.

This fictional case is inspired by recurring institutional challenges. Outcomes are possibilities, not predictions or assessments of leadership ability.
